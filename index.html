Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 November 2026 · Applies to the Android version of AFTERZERO: Zombie Frontier (package com.poyraz.afterzero).

Who we are

AFTERZERO: Zombie Frontier ("the Game") is developed and published by Poyraz Yazılım (Ömer Poyraz), Türkiye. Contact: omerr.poyrazz@gmail.com.

Summary

The Game works offline. We do not run any servers, we do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, e-mail, contacts, photos, location or any other personal information ourselves. The only third-party services built into the Game are Google AdMob (ads) and Google Play Billing (purchases). The Game contains no analytics and no tracking SDKs other than those two.

1. Data stored on your device

Your progress (level, scrap, upgrades, unlocked weapons, records, daily missions, purchase status) and your settings (volume, graphics, language, controls) are saved only in the Game's private storage on your device. This data never leaves your device and is not sent to us. Uninstalling the Game deletes it.

2. Advertising — Google AdMob

The free version shows banner ads (menus only), interstitial ads (between games only) and optional rewarded video ads (only when you choose to watch one). Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google's SDK may collect and process data such as:

DataPurpose
Advertising ID (Android), device and OS information, IP address (used for approximate location)Ad serving, frequency capping, fraud prevention, measurement
Ad interactions (impressions, clicks), app performance and crash data related to adsAd delivery and reporting

This data is collected directly by Google, not by us; we do not receive it in identifiable form. Google's processing is described in its Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland): before any ad is requested, the Game shows Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) where you can accept or refuse personalised advertising. You can reset your Advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads at any time in Android Settings → Google → Ads.

PRO and "Remove Ads" owners see no banner or interstitial ads.

3. In-app purchases — Google Play Billing

Optional purchases (AFTERZERO PRO subscriptions and lifetime, Remove Ads, Hero Pack, Weapon Pack, Double Scrap) are processed entirely by Google Play. We never see or store your payment card or billing details. The Game only receives from Google Play the list of products you own and a purchase token, which it uses to unlock content and to restore purchases on a new install. Subscriptions can be cancelled in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions. Nothing sold is required to progress in the Game.

4. Children

The Game contains cartoon combat with zombies and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the third-party services above, contact us and we will help you request deletion from Google.

5. Permissions

The Game requests only INTERNET and ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE (to load ads and connect to Google Play). The AdMob SDK declares com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID to read the Advertising ID.

6. Data retention and deletion

Local save data stays on your device until you uninstall the Game or clear its data. We hold no data about you on any server, so there is nothing for us to delete; for data processed by Google you can use your Google Account controls or contact us for help.

7. Security

Save files are written atomically with integrity checks to prevent corruption. All communication with Google services uses encrypted connections managed by Google's SDKs.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (e.g. GDPR, UK GDPR, KVKK in Türkiye, CCPA) you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to processing of personal data. Because we do not collect personal data ourselves, most requests concern Google; write to us and we will answer within 30 days.

9. Changes

If the Game adds or removes a service that processes data, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.
